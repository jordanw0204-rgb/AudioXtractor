A closer look.

A window that stays with you

Resize any edge of this window; its size and position are remembered. The pin next to Info keeps this window above other apps. The panel follows the last active website tab.

The original audio

Sources discovers page players, media links, metadata and network responses. Resolve page audio uses the local companion to find complete audio streams on supported websites. Resolved audio is saved without MP3 conversion; video sources are labeled. Protected or private players may not expose downloadable audio.

Drag audio into your workflow

Recordings prepare for dragging automatically. Drag a row into a folder or an app that accepts audio files. Check several rows to drag them together. Download selected and All export multiple files in one action.

YouTube conversion

Use current page or paste up to 50 individual video links. Two conversions run at once. Completed MP3s can be played, selected, dragged or downloaded. MP3 is lossy; WAV recording and original-source downloads remain separate. Some videos require sign-in or are unavailable.

Quick tab recording

Opening Recorder connects the current website and shows its live waveform. Start recording saves audio immediately; Stop & save keeps the tab ready for another take. No Share picker. Chrome can briefly interrupt playback when the tab first connects. Keeping that connection ready avoids another audio-routing change for each take. Disconnect tab, leaving Recorder while idle, or closing the idle window releases capture. An active recording can continue with this window closed.

Recording fidelity

Recordings preserve Chrome's captured samples in uncompressed 32-bit float WAV. Chrome may mix or resample before capture. Pause excludes that section. The panel can close while capture continues. Stop before closing the browser. Each WAV is limited to 2 GB or available storage.

Windows companion

Pinning, native dragging, resolution and conversion use a local .NET companion with your installed yt-dlp and FFmpeg. It is only accessible to this extension through Chrome native messaging. No cloud conversion service or microphone.
